# Rey

> Cast bible for the medieval channel (`skills/medieval-short`). **Look** and **Voice** come from the JSON.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
A white head with a gold five-point crown, a purple belted tunic and a long red cape with a fur collar. Medieval channel: any king (episode names him in the narration).

## Voice
ElevenLabs "Jose Almagro" (Spanish, peninsular). Delivery: slow, sure of himself; often says nothing at all.

## Personality
Authority with a fragile ego. Decides fast, listens late. Comic through silence and stares, never through slapstick on tragic episodes.

## Wants
To look like a king while things fall apart around him.

## Physical comedy
The long stare. Crossed arms. A slow turn of the head toward bad news.

## Catchphrases
- (silence)
- «¿Cómo dices?»

## Never
- A clown in a real tragedy.
- Put in modern clothes or with fantasy props in a historical episode.
