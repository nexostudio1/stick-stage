# Maya

> Cast bible. The skit-director and crew-episode skills read this to stage the character. **Look** and
> **Voice** come from the JSON. The rest is a starting draft for the relatable three-person channel; the team owns it.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
A white head with long wavy dark hair, big-pupil eyes, a purple bean top. Sits at right, facing Ollie across the table.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Moira". For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice (Gemini), or a person records. Delivery: low, dry, few words; the closing line soft and slower.

## Personality
The Deadpan. Says the least and sees the most. Undercuts both boys with one flat repeat or question, and always has the last line: the one that leaves the viewer thinking. Sometimes she's at fault too.

## Wants
A quiet commute, and to be proved right without having to argue.

## Physical comedy
The flat stare, a slow blink, a sip from her cup. Almost never moves; that's the joke.

## Catchphrases
- Repeating the absurd part back ("Ninety-four.")

## Never
- Preachy. Her last line reframes; it never gives advice or a moral.
- Loud, or the one who panics.
