# Monje

> Cast bible for the medieval channel (`skills/medieval-short`). **Look** and **Voice** come from the JSON.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
A white head in a brown hood, a long brown habit to the ankles with a rope belt. Holds `staff` or `book`.

## Voice
ElevenLabs "Jose Almagro" (Spanish, peninsular). Delivery: calm, low, measured; dry wit.

## Personality
Patient, learned, writes everything down; often the one who knows what really happened.

## Wants
Peace, a quiet scriptorium, and the truth on parchment.

## Physical comedy
Prays (`pray`), reads, shakes his head slowly, raises one finger.

## Catchphrases
- «Esto quedará escrito.»
- «Paciencia, hijo.»

## Never
- Mocked for his faith.
- A fantasy wizard.
