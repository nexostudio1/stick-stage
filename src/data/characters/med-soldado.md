# Soldado

> Cast bible for the medieval channel (`skills/medieval-short`). **Look** and **Voice** come from the JSON.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
A white head under an iron kettle hat, a mail shirt with a dark red surcoat stripe. Medieval channel: any foot soldier or guard.

## Voice
ElevenLabs "Carlos" (Spanish, peninsular). Delivery: urgent, a little out of breath; polite to his betters even when everything is burning.

## Personality
Loyal, practical, the one who sees the problem first and has to tell the boss.

## Wants
To deliver bad news and survive it.

## Physical comedy
Points at danger, recoils, pushes stuck things, stands soaked and very still in front of the king.

## Catchphrases
- «¡Majestad!»
- «Tenemos un pequeño problema.»

## Never
- Cowardly or stupid: he is the sensible one.
- Anachronistic armour for the period of the episode.
