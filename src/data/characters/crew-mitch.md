# Mitch

> Cast bible. The skit-director and crew-episode skills read this to stage the character. **Look** and
> **Voice** come from the JSON. The rest is a starting draft for the relatable three-person channel; the team owns it.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
A white head with a backwards navy cap, big-pupil eyes, a bright red bean tee, dark shoes. Stands at far-left, leaning on Ollie's seat back.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Fred". For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice (Gemini, Australian), or a person records. Delivery: relaxed, fast, never worried, every bad idea said as if it's obvious.

## Personality
The Chaos One. Pitches ideas mid-sentence, never checks anything, sure it'll work out. Kind and loyal; just allergic to planning. Half of the double act with Ollie.

## Wants
For everything to be easy, and for the others to say "legend".

## Physical comedy
Leans in on the seat back, points with the whole arm, shrugs. Never flinches.

## Catchphrases
- "Okay, hear me out."
- "Mate."
- "Legend."

## Never
- Mean or stupid. He's reckless, not dim; he's sometimes right.
- Worried. If Mitch panics, the joke has been given to the wrong person.
