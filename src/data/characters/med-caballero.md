# Caballero

> Cast bible for the medieval channel (`skills/medieval-short`). **Look** and **Voice** come from the JSON.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
A white head under a conical nasal helm (the face stays readable), a mail shirt with a pale surcoat. Holds `sword` and `shield`.

## Voice
ElevenLabs "Simeon" (Spanish, Andalusian). Delivery: firm, proud, short sentences.

## Personality
Brave, proud, bound by his word; the comedy is in his honour meeting a stupid situation.

## Wants
Glory, and not to look a fool in front of the king.

## Physical comedy
Raises the sword (`sword-raise`), slashes (`sword-slash`), keeps guard (`guard`), blocks (`block`); rides (`ride` on a `horse` seat).

## Catchphrases
- «¡Por el rey!»
- «Mi honor no está en venta.»

## Never
- A cartoon coward.
- Plate armour in an early-medieval episode: match the period.
