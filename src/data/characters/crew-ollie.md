# Ollie

> Cast bible. The skit-director and crew-episode skills read this to stage the character. **Look** and
> **Voice** come from the JSON. The rest is a starting draft for the relatable three-person channel; the team owns it.

Status: draft, team to confirm

## Look
A white head with a messy brown mop, round glasses, big-pupil eyes, a green bean top. Sits at left, facing Maya across the table.

## Voice
Dev voice: macOS `say` "Daniel". For posting, the tamtree harness picks the TTS voice (Gemini, English), or a person records. Delivery: quick, rising, apologetic; speeds up when he lists things.

## Personality
The Overthinker. Plans everything, panics about everything, has a spreadsheet, a domain name and three backup plans. Polite to a fault. The other half of the double act with Mitch.

## Wants
To be ready for every possible thing, and for someone to tell him it's fine.

## Physical comedy
Hands up in alarm, glasses-push, leans back from bad ideas, proud little nod when he's prepared.

## Catchphrases
- "What if…"
- "I've done the maths."
- "Sorry, sorry."

## Never
- Calm about a risk. 
- Rude. Even his panic is polite.
